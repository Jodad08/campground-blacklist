# Campground Blacklist

**A quick and easy way to see if your new customer isn't wanted back.**

🔗 **Live app:** [campground-blacklist.marketingmeteor.workers.dev](https://campground-blacklist.marketingmeteor.workers.dev)

---

## What it is

Campground Blacklist is a shared reference tool for campground and RV‑park operators.
Before you check a new guest in, look them up against a community‑maintained list of
people who have been flagged at other parks — so you can spot a problem guest *before*
they pull onto your site, not after.

The whole idea is speed and simplicity: search a name, get an answer, make the call.

## Who it's for

- Campground and RV‑park owners and managers
- Front‑desk and reservation staff
- Anyone who takes bookings and wants a heads‑up on repeat problem guests

## Why it exists

A single bad guest can mean property damage, unpaid stays, disruptive behavior, or
safety concerns. Operators already talk to each other about who to watch out for —
this puts that shared knowledge in one place you can check in seconds.
